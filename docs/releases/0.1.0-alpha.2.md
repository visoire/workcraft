# WorkCraft 0.1.0-alpha.2

A simpler workspace, a first-start page, a guided cloud setup and newer app builds.

**Simpler to use**

- A panel has two buttons: Maximize and X. X only hides the panel; closing for good and freeing the memory is a red entry in the right-click menu (long press on a touch screen).
- A leaner top bar (one AI button, Settings at the top right), a tool rail whose icons you can drag into your own order, a short persona menu, a chat that greets you with one line instead of option cards, and five sections up front in Settings with everything else under Developer options.

**First start**

- A card in three steps, before anything loads: a welcome (with a way to support the project), the workspace, and where your files are saved. It is a landscape card on a computer, a smaller one on an iPad and the whole screen on a phone. The answer is kept in the browser and never asked again.

**Cloud storage, the simple way**

- "Save in the cloud" (main menu, command palette, Settings): pick Google Drive, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, MEGA, Proton Drive or any other service, read three short steps, press one button. No password is typed into WorkCraft: the service's own app syncs a folder, WorkCraft keeps a copy of the project in it. On a computer the folder is found for you; in Chrome and Edge a folder picker opens once; on iPad, iPhone, Safari and Firefox the project goes out as a ZIP through the share sheet ("Save to Files" lists iCloud Drive and every cloud app). A ZIP is always offered as the other way.

**Apps**

- MailCraft 0.13.0 (the 0.12.0 web build crashed in the browser; this one starts and works), SolidCraft 0.43.0, LightCraft Classic 26.47.1 (still stops at startup, an upstream fault that is documented).

**Fixed**

- Team configuration had two scrollbars.
- WordCraft and the other egui apps could stay black until a click; the frame layer now asks them for a frame in the first seconds. The black screen could not be made to appear on demand, so this is a mitigation, not a proven fix.

**Be aware:** this is an alpha. The cloud folder finder is tested with a fake disk, not with a real Dropbox, OneDrive, MEGA or Proton Drive folder; the iPad share sheet and Move up and Move down on a real iPad are not tested.
