# WorkCraft 0.1.0-alpha.1

The first public alpha of WorkCraft: a local-first workspace for documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs, with an AI assistant that works across every file.

**Two ways to use it**

- **In the browser, nothing to install:** [workcraft.cc/app](https://workcraft.cc/app/). Your files stay in your browser.
- **On your computer:** `git clone`, `npm install`, `npm run dev` (Node.js 22 or newer). The terminal, local models (Ollama) and the desktop apps need this way. See [the README](https://github.com/visoire/workcraft#quick-start).

**What is in it:** WordCraft, GridCraft, DeckCraft and PdfCraft as panels of one window; a catalog of 44 apps in ready-made workspaces; an assistant with Team Mode, one specialist per app and an MCP server; local models or your own keys; Google Drive and folder sync; light and dark mode that follows one switch; iPadOS with Apple Pencil support; English and German.

**Be aware:** this is an alpha. iPadOS support is built and tested in WebKit at iPad sizes, not on a physical iPad. Google Drive sync has not been tested against a real account in this release. Third-party apps beyond the four office apps are fetched separately and vary in quality.

Full details, what is checked and what is not: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/visoire/workcraft/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). Found something? [Open an issue](https://github.com/visoire/workcraft/issues/new/choose).
